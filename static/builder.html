How the numbers are estimated

Expected views: average views of their paid posts with us; if we haven't paid them, the median of their recent posts. Price: what you type, else their quoted price, else what we paid per post before.

Registrations = expected views × registrations per 1K views of the waves you pick above. API users = registrations × that benchmark's reg → API rate. These assume the new wave converts like the benchmark did — treat them as a range, not a promise.

Creators missing views or a price are left out of CPM and cost per registration (shown as a warning).
