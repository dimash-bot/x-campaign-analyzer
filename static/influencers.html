Median views / ER (their normal): median over their last ~30 own posts, excluding the ones we paid for. Views (with us): total views of all our paid posts with them (hover for the per-post average). vs median: avg views of our paid post ÷ their median (above 1× = our post beat their usual post). Reach: avg views of our post ÷ followers. $ / 1K followers: price per post ÷ followers × 1,000 (rate-card benchmark). CPM, ER and CPE only count posts that have views. Quality: checks on the account itself (following ÷ followers, account age vs followers, posts a day, original posts in 90 days, median views ÷ followers, their ER, replies per like, viral-only reach, boosted posts) plus, once we've paid them, how our post did (ER, replies per like, CPM, views vs their usual). Bot followers: of their 200 newest followers, the share with 3+ fake-account signals (new account, default avatar, no bio, ≤5 posts, <10 followers, follows 20× more than follow it, auto-generated handle). Typical is ~28%; over 40% is a warning, over 50% a hard fail. Add influencers to check

Paste X accounts — @handles or x.com links, one per line or separated by commas. Each one gets its profile and recent posts pulled and a quality verdict. Post links count as their recent posts. Add the price they quote with ✎ on the row afterwards. They show as "not paid yet" until you add one of their posts to a wave.
