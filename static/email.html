Where the spam rate comes from — and how to get Gmail's

On this page: Resend's email.complained events. They exist when a mailbox provider runs a “feedback loop”: Yahoo, AOL, Outlook / Hotmail and some others tell the sender each time someone clicks “Report spam”. These come in through the API sync and, faster, through the webhook.

Gmail never reports single complaints, and most of your recipients are on Gmail, so the number here undercounts. Gmail's spam rate lives in Google Postmaster Tools — the same number Google uses to decide inbox vs. spam:

  1. Open postmaster.google.com with a Google account → Add domain → enter the sending domain (e.g. drex-usa.com, nace.ai).
  2. Google gives a TXT record; add it in the domain's DNS and click Verify.
  3. Data appears after ~a day, and only on days you send roughly 100+ emails to Gmail addresses. Look at Spam rate (keep under 0.1%, never 0.3%+), plus Domain reputation and Authentication (SPF / DKIM / DMARC).

Microsoft (Outlook / Hotmail): sign up for SNDS (sendersupport.olc.protection.outlook.com/snds) to see complaint rates per sending IP — with Resend you share IPs unless you have a dedicated one, so this matters less.

Postmaster Tools also has an API, so once a domain is verified this page could pull Gmail's daily spam rate automatically.
